Swimming Learning Program

A structured path from complete beginner (water-fearful) to a confident, efficient swimmer across all four strokes. Tutorials in English and Tamil.

Swimmer Level Progression

  • Level 1 Water comfort, floating, basic breathing
  • Level 2 Can swim 25m freestyle with basic form
  • Level 3 Comfortable with freestyle & backstroke, 200m continuous
  • Level 4 All four strokes, flip turns, 500m+ continuous
  • Level 5 Efficient technique, endurance, open water capable

Program Overview

  1. Phase 1: Water Comfort & Safety (Weeks 1-3) — Overcome Fear
  2. Phase 2: Floating & Breathing (Weeks 4-6) — Foundation Skills
  3. Phase 3: Freestyle (Front Crawl) (Weeks 7-11) — Your First Stroke
  4. Phase 4: Backstroke & Breaststroke (Weeks 12-17) — Expanding Your Arsenal
  5. Phase 5: Butterfly & Endurance (Weeks 18-24) — Complete Swimmer
  6. Phase 6: Efficiency & Open Water (Weeks 25-30) — Advanced

Safety First

Critical Rules: Never swim alone as a beginner. Always practice in a pool with a lifeguard or experienced swimmer present. Learn where the depth markers are. Know your limits — fatigue in water is dangerous.
🛌

Never Swim Alone

Always have a buddy or swim where a lifeguard is present.

⚠️

Know the Depth

Start in shallow water (waist/chest deep). Move deeper only when ready.

💧

No Panic

If tired, flip to your back and float. Never thrash — relax and breathe.

🩹

Warm Up

Always warm up before swimming. Never jump into cold water suddenly.

Essential Equipment

🥼

Swim Goggles

Anti-fog, UV protection. Essential for seeing underwater and proper technique.

👙

Swimsuit

Fitted swimsuit (jammers for men, one-piece for women). No loose clothing.

🏊

Swim Cap

Silicone cap reduces drag, protects hair from chlorine.

🔷

Kickboard

For kick drills. Most pools provide them. Essential for beginners.

🟦

Pull Buoy

Placed between legs to isolate upper body. Used from Phase 3 onwards.

🔳

Fins (Optional)

Short fins help with kick technique and body position. Great training tool.

Water Comfort & Safety
Goal: Overcome fear, become comfortable in water
Weeks 1-3

Week 1: Getting In & Water Familiarization

Enter and exit the pool safely (ladder, steps)
Walk in waist-deep water comfortably
Submerge face in water (eyes open with goggles)
Blow bubbles with mouth and nose underwater
Bob up and down (chin to forehead underwater)
Hold the pool wall and kick gently

Practice (20-30 min, 3-4x per week)

  • Bobbing: 20 bobs — inhale above water, exhale through nose underwater
  • Face submersion: hold for 3 seconds, build to 10 seconds
  • Wall kicks: hold gutter, body horizontal, flutter kick 30 seconds
  • Walk the width of the pool and back (shallow end only)

Watch These Tutorials:

▶ Overcome Water Fear ▶ Breathing Basics ▶ நீச்சல் பயம் போக்க (Tamil) ▶ First Day in Pool
Key Mindset: Fear of water is completely normal. Go at YOUR pace. The goal this week is simply to feel relaxed in the water, not to swim. Spend as long as you need here before moving on.

Week 2: Submersion & Breath Control

Full head submersion for 5-10 seconds
Open eyes underwater with goggles
Rhythmic breathing: inhale mouth, exhale nose underwater
Pick up objects from pool floor (shallow end)
Push off wall and glide (with face in water)
Seated jump into chest-deep water

Practice

  • Rhythmic bobbing: 30 bobs with proper inhale/exhale timing
  • Mushroom float: tuck knees to chest, float face-down for 5 seconds
  • Wall push-off glide: push, streamline arms, glide until you stop (face in water)
  • Treasure hunt: retrieve 3-5 objects from shallow pool floor

Watch These Tutorials:

▶ Breath Control Drills ▶ Push & Glide ▶ Beginner Lessons (Tamil)

Week 3: Floating & Body Position

Front float (starfish) — 10 seconds unsupported
Back float — 10 seconds unsupported
Recovery from float to standing position
Streamline position (arms overhead, body straight)
Glide on front with kick (5 meters)
Glide on back with kick (5 meters)

Practice

  • Starfish float: arms and legs spread, relax completely, 10-15 seconds
  • Back float: ears in water, hips up, eyes to ceiling, relax 15 seconds
  • Glide + kick: push off wall, streamline, add flutter kick for 5m
  • Float recovery: from front float, tuck knees, press down hands, stand up

Watch These Tutorials:

▶ Back Float Tutorial ▶ Front Float ▶ Streamline Position ▶ மிதப்பது எப்படி (Tamil)
Floating Secret: The key to floating is RELAXATION. Tense muscles sink. Fill your lungs (they're your built-in life jacket), look up at the ceiling for back float, and let your body do the work. It may take several sessions — that's normal.

Phase 1 Milestone Checklist

  • Comfortable putting entire face/head underwater for 10+ seconds
  • Can blow bubbles continuously through nose while submerged
  • Float on front and back for 10 seconds without assistance
  • Push off wall and glide 3-5 meters in streamline
  • No panic response when water touches face
  • Can recover from float to standing safely

Kicking & Breathing Foundation
Goal: Propulsion + rhythmic breathing
Weeks 4-6

Week 4: Flutter Kick Mastery

Flutter kick technique (from hips, not knees)
Kick with kickboard — 25m without stopping
Kick on back (arms at side) — 25m
Kick tempo: small, fast kicks (not big splashy ones)
Maintain horizontal body position while kicking
Kick without kickboard (arms in streamline)

Practice (30-40 min, 3-4x per week)

  • Kickboard lengths: 4 x 25m with 30-second rest between
  • Back kick: 4 x 25m, arms at sides, face up, gentle kick
  • Vertical kicking: in deep end (if comfortable), kick to stay afloat 30 seconds
  • Streamline kick (no board): push off, arms forward, kick 10-15m

Watch These Tutorials:

▶ Flutter Kick Technique ▶ Kick From Hips ▶ Kickboard Drills ▶ Flutter Kick (Tamil)

Week 5: Side Breathing

Exhale underwater continuously (not holding breath)
Turn head to side to inhale (one goggle in, one out)
Breathing timing with kick (breathe every 3-4 kicks)
Practice on both sides (bilateral breathing)
Avoid lifting head forward to breathe
Combine: kick + side breathing for 10m

Practice

  • Side breathing at wall: hold wall, face in water, rotate head to breathe, repeat 20x
  • Kickboard one-arm: hold board with one arm, other at side, breathe to free side
  • 6-3-6 drill: 6 kicks on side (face in), 3 strokes, 6 kicks on other side
  • Walk and breathe: walk in shallow water, practice head-turn rhythm

Watch These Tutorials:

▶ Side Breathing ▶ Bilateral Breathing ▶ Breathing Mistakes ▶ மூச்சு பயிற்சி (Tamil)
Common Mistake: Holding your breath underwater causes CO2 buildup and panic. ALWAYS exhale continuously through your nose when your face is in the water. Inhale only when you turn your head to the side.

Week 6: Arm Movements Introduction

Freestyle arm entry: fingertips first, shoulder width
Catch phase: hand angles down, elbow high
Pull phase: pull water past hip
Recovery: elbow leads, relaxed arm over water
Single-arm drill with kickboard
Doggy paddle to freestyle transition

Practice

  • Single-arm freestyle: kickboard in one hand, stroke with other, 4 x 25m each side
  • Catch-up drill: both arms forward, one strokes while other waits at front
  • Standing arm circles: practice arm motion on land, visualize entry and pull
  • Pull buoy freestyle arms only: isolate upper body, 4 x 25m

Watch These Tutorials:

▶ Freestyle Arms ▶ High Elbow Catch ▶ Catch-Up Drill ▶ Hand Entry

Phase 2 Milestone Checklist

  • Can kick 25m with kickboard without stopping
  • Rhythmic side breathing is natural (no panic, no lifting head)
  • Understand and can demonstrate freestyle arm motion
  • Can combine kick + breathing for 10-15 meters
  • Back kick for 25m comfortably
3
Freestyle (Front Crawl)
Goal: Swim 50-100m continuous freestyle
Weeks 7-11

Weeks 7-8: Putting It All Together

Full freestyle: arms + kick + breathing combined
Breathing every 3 strokes (bilateral)
Body rotation (hips and shoulders roll together)
Swim 25m freestyle without stopping
Streamline push-off at each wall
Pacing: swim at 60% effort, not sprinting

Practice (40-45 min, 3-4x per week)

  • Warm-up: 4 x 25m kick (with board)
  • Main set: 8 x 25m freestyle with 20-second rest (focus on one element each rep)
  • Drill: 4 x 25m catch-up or fingertip drag
  • Cool-down: 100m easy backstroke kick

Watch These Tutorials:

▶ Full Freestyle ▶ Body Rotation ▶ Skills NT: Freestyle ▶ Freestyle (Tamil)

Weeks 9-10: Building Distance & Efficiency

Swim 50m continuous (2 lengths) freestyle
Reduce stroke count per length (efficiency)
Open turn at the wall (touch, turn, push off)
Maintain form when tired
Fingertip drag drill (high elbow recovery)
Swim 100m with short rests at walls

Practice

  • Build set: 25m easy, 25m moderate, 25m strong, 25m easy — repeat 2x
  • Stroke counting: count strokes per 25m, try to reduce by 1-2 each week
  • Fingertip drag: 4 x 25m (forces high elbow recovery)
  • Continuous swim: aim for 100m (take 10-sec rest at walls if needed)

Watch These Tutorials:

▶ Build Endurance ▶ Stroke Efficiency ▶ Fingertip Drag Drill ▶ Open Turn

Week 11: Freestyle Refinement

Swim 200m continuous freestyle
Two-beat kick vs. six-beat kick understanding
Hand entry: avoid crossing center line
Head position: look at pool bottom, not forward
Relaxed recovery arm (not tense/stiff)
Even pacing across multiple lengths

Practice

  • Warm-up: 200m mixed (kick, pull, swim)
  • Technique focus: 6 x 50m freestyle, alternate drill/swim by 25
  • Continuous swim: 200m at conversational pace
  • Cool-down: 100m easy choice
  • Total session volume: 600-800m

Phase 3 Milestone Checklist

  • Swim 200m continuous freestyle without stopping
  • Bilateral breathing (every 3 strokes) is comfortable
  • Stroke count is under 20 per 25m
  • Can do open turns efficiently at each wall
  • No longer feel "out of breath" after 50m
  • Body stays horizontal (hips don't sink)
4
Backstroke & Breaststroke
Goal: Two additional strokes, 400m+ sessions
Weeks 12-17

Weeks 12-14: Backstroke

Back float with flutter kick (relaxed, face up)
Backstroke arm technique: pinky enters first
Alternating arm recovery (straight arm over)
Body rotation on backstroke (shoulder roll)
Head position: still, looking straight up
Backstroke flags awareness (5m from wall)
Backstroke turn (roll to front, open turn)
Swim 50m continuous backstroke

Practice

  • Single arm backstroke: one arm at side, other strokes, 4 x 25m each
  • 6-kick switch: 6 kicks on one side, switch arms, 6 kicks other side
  • Cup of water drill: balance imaginary cup on forehead (head still)
  • Full backstroke: 6 x 25m with focus on smooth entry and rotation
  • Mixed set: 25m freestyle + 25m backstroke, 4 rounds

Watch These Tutorials:

▶ Backstroke Basics ▶ Arm Technique ▶ Body Rotation ▶ Backstroke (Tamil) 📷 #Backstroke

Weeks 15-17: Breaststroke

Breaststroke kick (whip kick / frog kick)
Kick timing: heels to butt, turn feet out, snap together
Arm pull: heart-shaped pull, elbows stay forward
Timing: pull, breathe, kick, glide
Glide phase (streamline between strokes)
Head position: forward breathing (not lifting high)
Breaststroke pullout (underwater after turn)
Swim 50m continuous breaststroke

Practice

  • Kick on back (hands on knees): practice whip kick motion, 4 x 25m
  • 2 kicks + 1 pull: extra glide phase, feel the timing
  • Breaststroke arms with flutter kick: isolate arm motion, 4 x 25m
  • Full breaststroke with long glide: count strokes (aim for under 8 per 25m)
  • Breaststroke pullout: push off wall, 1 pull + 1 kick underwater before surfacing

Watch These Tutorials:

▶ Breaststroke Kick ▶ Timing & Glide ▶ Common Mistakes ▶ Pullout Technique ▶ தவளை நீச்சல் (Tamil)
Breaststroke Key: The power comes from the KICK, not the arms. The arm pull is just for breathing and a little forward motion. Focus on a strong whip kick and a long glide. "Pull, Breathe, Kick, Gliiiiide."

Phase 4 Milestone Checklist

  • Swim 50m backstroke with proper rotation and steady head
  • Swim 50m breaststroke with correct whip kick timing
  • Total session volume: 800-1200m
  • Can swim 400m mixing freestyle, backstroke, and breaststroke
  • Comfortable swimming in the deep end for extended periods
  • Understanding of each stroke's timing and rhythm
5
Butterfly & Endurance
Goal: All 4 strokes, 1000m+ sessions
Weeks 18-24

Weeks 18-20: Butterfly Stroke

Dolphin kick: undulation from chest, not knees
Body wave: chest press down, hips rise
Butterfly arm recovery: both arms over simultaneously
Timing: 2 kicks per arm cycle (kick on entry, kick on pull)
Breathing: forward (chin on water surface)
Swim 25m butterfly (even if exhausting)

Practice

  • Dolphin kick on front: arms at sides, undulate 4 x 25m
  • Dolphin kick underwater: streamline, 3-5 kicks off each wall
  • Single arm butterfly: one arm strokes, other at side, dolphin kick
  • 3-3-3 drill: 3 strokes right arm, 3 strokes left arm, 3 full butterfly
  • Full butterfly: 4 x 25m (rest as long as needed between)

Watch These Tutorials:

▶ Butterfly Basics ▶ Dolphin Kick ▶ Butterfly Timing ▶ Single Arm Drill ▶ Butterfly (Tamil)
Butterfly Reality Check: Butterfly is the hardest stroke and the most tiring. It's NORMAL to only manage 25m at first. Focus on the rhythm and undulation, not speed. Many accomplished swimmers only swim butterfly in short bursts.

Weeks 21-22: Individual Medley (IM) & Flip Turns

Swim a 100m IM (25 fly, 25 back, 25 breast, 25 free)
Freestyle flip turn (somersault at wall)
Backstroke flip turn
Transition between strokes (IM turns)
Underwater streamline off each wall (3-5m)
Swim 200m IM

Practice

  • Flip turn progression: somersault in middle of pool, then near wall, then with push-off
  • Approach drill: swim into wall, flip, push off in streamline
  • 100 IM: focus on smooth transitions between strokes
  • 200 IM build: increase IM distance over 2 weeks

▶ Flip Turn Tutorial ▶ IM Transitions ▶ Backstroke Flip Turn ▶ Underwater Push-Off

Weeks 23-24: Endurance Building

Swim 1000m continuous (mix of strokes)
Pacing strategy: negative split (second half faster)
Interval training: 10 x 50m on a set time
Active recovery (easy 50m between hard sets)
Session structure: warm-up, main set, cool-down
Total session volume: 1500-2000m

Sample Workout (1500m)

  • Warm-up: 200m easy free + 100m kick + 100m drill
  • Pre-set: 4 x 50m (25 drill + 25 swim) on 1:00
  • Main set: 10 x 50m freestyle on 1:10 (aim consistent pace)
  • Variety: 4 x 25m each stroke (100m fly, 100m back, 100m breast)
  • Cool-down: 200m easy choice

  • Can swim all four strokes (even if butterfly is only 25-50m)

6
Efficiency & Open Water
Goal: Refined technique, open water readiness
Weeks 25-30

Weeks 25-27: Technique Refinement

Freestyle: stroke rate vs. distance per stroke optimization
Early vertical forearm (EVF) catch
Streamlined push-offs: 5+ meters underwater
Reduce drag: tight streamline, pointed toes
Bilateral breathing every 3 or 5 strokes
Video analysis of your own stroke

Practice

  • SWOLF score: count strokes + time per 25m — try to lower the total
  • Sculling drills: feel the water with hands at different positions
  • Tarzan drill (head-up freestyle): builds catch strength
  • Fist drill: swim with closed fists (improves forearm catch)
  • Record yourself: use a waterproof camera or ask someone poolside

Watch These Tutorials:

▶ EVF Catch ▶ Reduce Drag ▶ Sculling Drills ▶ Stroke Analysis ▶ SWOLF Explained

Weeks 28-30: Open Water Skills & Confidence

Sighting: head-up look every 6-8 strokes
Swimming without walls (no push-offs to rest)
Treading water: 5+ minutes continuously
Swimming in a straight line without lane markers
Dealing with waves/chop (if practicing in open water)
Wetsuit swimming (different buoyancy)
Continuous 2000m swim
Basic water rescue awareness (reach, throw, don't go)

Practice

  • Sighting drill in pool: head-up freestyle every 8 strokes, maintain pace
  • Treading water: mix egg-beater kick and sculling for 5 minutes
  • Swim without touching walls: turn before the wall (simulates open water)
  • Open water (supervised): swim along shore, with buddy, in calm conditions
  • Distance build: weekly long swim increasing to 2000m continuous

Watch These Tutorials:

▶ Open Water Tips ▶ Sighting Technique ▶ Treading Water ▶ Pool to Open Water ▶ OW Safety 📷 #OpenWaterSwimming
Open Water Safety: NEVER swim in open water alone. Always swim parallel to shore, not away from it. Wear a bright swim buoy for visibility. Check conditions (currents, temperature, jellyfish) before entering. Start in calm, shallow, supervised areas.

Phase 6 — You're a Confident Swimmer When:

  • Swim 2000m continuous without difficulty
  • All four strokes are comfortable and efficient
  • Flip turns are automatic on freestyle and backstroke
  • Can tread water for 5+ minutes
  • Comfortable in deep water with no wall nearby
  • Can sight and swim straight without lane markers
  • Session volume: 2000-3000m
  • Ready for open water swimming with proper safety measures
  • Could participate in a beginner triathlon swim leg (750m)

Key Principles Throughout Your Journey

💨

Exhale Underwater

The #1 beginner mistake is holding breath. Always blow out underwater, inhale above.

🧖

Relax to Float

Tension sinks you. The more relaxed you are, the better you float and swim.

🐠

Smooth = Fast

Reducing drag is more important than adding power. Glide more, splash less.

📈

Consistency Wins

3-4 shorter sessions per week beats one long session. Frequency builds muscle memory.
