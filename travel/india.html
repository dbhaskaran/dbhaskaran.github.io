Temples of South India (Tamil Nadu)

Shree Maha Periyava Dharisitha 100 Koyilkal

Temples visited and recommended by Sri Mahaperiyava — one line each, with a map link. More entries coming as the full list is added.

  1. Sri Kanchi Kamakshi Amman Temple

    Kamakshi Amman Temple in Kanchipuram is traditionally treated as Mahaperiyava’s most beloved temple, where he is said to have dedicated his life to Kamakshi Amman’s worship.

    Google Maps

  2. Sri Pasupatheeswarar Temple Pandhanallur

    Mahaperiyava is remembered for emphasizing the temple’s ancient Shaiva identity, especially its link to the Tevaram tradition and Chola-era worship.

    Google Maps

  3. Arulmigu Sri Aarudra Kabaaleeswarar Temple, Erode Fort

    A distinct Mahaperiyava-related tradition is that he brought attention to lesser-known Shiva temples by treating them as spiritually powerful even when they were not famous.

    Google Maps

  4. Magizhancheri Sri Unmatha Nataraja

    This temple is often mentioned in Mahaperiyava circles as one of the quiet, hidden shrines whose darshan he inspired devotees to value deeply.

    Google Maps

  5. Thiruvanikka Sri Panchamukeshwarar

    A unique Mahaperiyava-style teaching connected to such temples is that even small or remote Shiva shrines can be as sacred as major pilgrim centers.

    Google Maps

  6. Thirutani Sri Subramania Swami

    Mahaperiyava devotees especially remember Tiruttani because of the moving story that he referred to Murugan here as bringing peace after battle, making the hill temple feel like a place of spiritual cooling.

    Google Maps

  7. Arulmigu Sri Jurahareswarar Temple

    Maha Periyava miraculously cured a devotee's recurring high fever by taking him to the Jwarahareswara and Kachchabeswarar temples, where he instructed Vedic scholars to chant the Surya Shatakam (a set of 100 verses praising the Sun God) for the devotee to hear.

    Google Maps
